## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Observations section is full-width (880px)
The buddy observations section SHALL be a full-width card (880×120 rx=12 fill=#fff stroke=#e2e8f0), not constrained to the right sidebar (SVG line 191).

### Requirement: Each observation card has a "觉" avatar circle
Each observation card (rx=8 fill=#fafafa stroke=#f1f5f9) SHALL start with a "觉" avatar: a purple circle (r=6 fill=#ede9fe) containing the character "觉" (8px/#7c3aed textAnchor=middle) (SVG lines 197-198, 204-205, 211-212).

### Requirement: Observation text is inline quotation format
The observation text SHALL be rendered inline with the avatar: quoted text (11px/#0f172a) followed by date and mastery_hint on the next line (9px/#94a3b8) (SVG lines 199-200).

### Requirement: Up to 3 observation cards with specific widths
Observation 1: 410×32 (left), Observation 2: 426×32 (right), Observation 3: 848×28 (full-width bottom) (SVG lines 197, 203, 210).
